# Mandatory attribution

ATAS is distributed under the **ATAS Attribution License 1.0** (`LicenseRef-ATAS-Attribution-1.0`).

Use of ATAS requires credit to the original project and author. This applies to source and binary redistribution, production deployments, demonstrations, hosted services, integrations and derivative or rebranded works.

## Minimum required credit

> ATAS — Added Time Assistance System. Created by José Francisco Bustamante Ocampo. Copyright 2026 José Francisco Bustamante Ocampo and ATAS contributors.

## Where the credit must appear

For software with a user-facing interface, place the credit in a reasonably accessible **About, Credits, Legal, System Information, acknowledgements page, startup banner, or equivalent location**. It does not need to occupy every screen.

For headless, embedded, API-only or internal systems, include it in operator documentation and at least one discoverable runtime or machine surface such as startup output, generated API documentation, a legal/about endpoint, or equivalent system information.

Redistributions must also preserve `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, applicable copyright notices and a clear indication of modifications to ATAS-derived files.

Rebranding does **not** remove the attribution requirement.

The required credit must not imply endorsement, FIFA/IFAB certification, affiliation, or permission to use third-party trademarks.
